Provide a production interactive Avalonia `FsusSlider` (#659) with rendered
track, fill, and thumb; pointer capture and drag commit behavior; arrow,
Home/End, and PageUp/PageDown keyboard operation; bindable range, value,
disabled, accessible-name, and accessible-value properties; value changed and
commit events; and a RangeValue automation peer. The slider template covers
Light, Dark, high-contrast, density, focus, disabled, and reduced-motion states.
